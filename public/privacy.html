Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to information when you visit this portfolio or use its contact form.

Contact messages

The contact form asks for your name and email address, and may ask for a company name, project or opportunity type, budget range and message.

When you submit the form, this site opens your device’s email app with a draft addressed to me. The site does not send that draft automatically. If you choose to send it, your email app and email provider deliver the information to my inbox. I use it to respond to your inquiry and related follow-up, and keep correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for that purpose or a legal obligation.

You can contact me at hemanthsaikumar551@gmail.com to ask about a message you sent.

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google’s font services to retrieve them, and Google may receive technical information associated with that request. See Google’s privacy policy.

This portfolio does not intentionally use analytics or advertising trackers. Vercel, Google, and your email provider handle information under their own policies. Their services may process information in other countries.

Your choices

You do not have to send a message. If you use the form, review the draft in your email app and send it only if you want to. You can also contact me directly using the email address above.

Security and updates

The site uses HTTPS and standard safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. This notice may be updated if the site’s data practices change; the latest revision date appears at the top.
